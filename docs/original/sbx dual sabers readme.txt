SBX DUAL SABERS (TEST VERSION)
An add-on for SBX-JK v3.1 by ShadowX

WHAT IT DOES
The Sith lightsaber gets a third stance: Boc's, with a single saber in each hand. Any player skin can use it.
- Boc's stance and swings (stand, chop, cut, block) are converted to your own skeleton from your own copy of Jedi Knight when a level loads, so no LucasArts animation is shipped.
- Only Boc's upper body is used when you move: your legs walk, run, strafe, turn, crouch and jump with SBX's own animations while your arms keep twirling the two sabers as Boc does standing, at his own pace however fast you move, carrying on from one move to the next. His chop, cut and block move only the upper body, so you can swing while you walk. Standing still, you crouch like Boc and twirl the sabers as he does.
- Boc twists his whole upper body hard as he chops and cuts. Your torso follows a steadier version of it: his turns smoothed out and 60% as far, while your arms and sabers still swing where his do.
- Each hand holds its saber by the middle of the hilt.
- The attacks while backing up or sliding left or right, and the heavy cut, are the twin saber attacks from EAH_CL0wN's Sith Sabers, used with permission. Two fixes in the heavy cut: the left arm kept turning after its last move and swung that blade behind you and through your hips (it now comes back to guard and holds), and two thirds of the way through, the left blade snapped about 55 degrees in an instant (it now swings there).
- The attack while walking or running forward is this add-on's own twin cross-cut: both sabers come up beside your head and over your shoulders, swing over to point straight ahead, and cut down and inward, meeting at the tips and crossing halfway along in an X in front of you, then come back to guard. It sweeps through the space right in front of you, so it hits someone there. It moves only the upper body, so your legs keep walking or running, and it starts and ends in Boc's stance, so it blends in and out of it. On SBX's Kyle the blades stay clear of the head and body all the way through, over SBX's walk, run and crouch-walk (a skin with a much bigger body could still touch).
- Attacking while you stand and turn fast (drag the mouse left or right) does a twin spinning slash that way, like SBX's lightsaber spinning slash: SBX's spin and legs, with both arms swinging the Sith Sabers cut for that way.

The Sith lightsaber also gets a fourth stance: the reverse grip. You hold the two sabers blade-down, each blade leaving the little-finger side of your fist and running back along your forearm, as Starkiller and Ahsoka hold theirs.
- It has its own stand. As in Boc's stance, your legs walk, run, strafe, turn, crouch and jump with SBX's own animations while your upper body keeps the stand.
- Its attacks are its own. Fire standing still: a right hook, a left hook and an X across the chest, then hooks across the chest. Fire moving forward: a right hook and a left hook, while your legs keep walking or running. Moving left or right: a slash, then a twin sweep that way. Moving back: a rising scissor catch. Standing and turning fast: a cyclone that way.
- Second fire: a quick coil, then a hop as high as a normal jump with a whole turn in the air.
- Running flat out for about half a second, it breaks into a dash, until you slow down. The dash is how you run, not a speed boost.
- Its moves are made from your own copy of Jedi Knight when a level loads, like Boc's.

WHAT YOU NEED
- SBX-JK v3.1 (SBX-JK v31.gob). Nothing else: Continuum and other skin packs are optional (see GOOD TO KNOW).
- A browser version of Jedi Knight that converts the animations as the level loads.
  - The site at https://jkdf2mp.jkdf2mp-lobby.workers.dev/ does everything here, the reverse grip and footsteps included, since 25 October 2026 (its main menu shows 8ccc356c in the corner, or a later version). If it still says "No reverse grip in this game.", reload the page.
  - An older browser version, such as the site from 22 October 2026 until 25 October, makes Boc's moves but not the reverse grip's. There the Lightsaber Stance key goes staff, Sariss, Boc's, staff (the first time in a level it skips the reverse grip, the game says "No reverse grip in this game."), and walking makes no footstep sounds.
- In the original JK.EXE and in other OpenJKDF2 builds it loads, and you play SBX as usual without Boc's stance and the reverse grip: the Lightsaber Stance key goes staff, Sariss, staff. The Dual Sabers key can't be bound at all: Setup says "Unable to bind control to this action", because those games have room for only 20 action keys (see GOOD TO KNOW), so removing other keys won't help. The first time in a level the Lightsaber Stance key skips Boc's stance, the game says "No Boc's stance or reverse grip in this game."

INSTALL
Put SBX Dual Sabers.gob in the same folder as SBX-JK v31.gob, wherever that is (Resource, or a mod folder such as expansions\SBX), and play with both ticked. In the browser version's list of mods, SBX Dual Sabers has to be above SBX-JK v31.
Don't put it in Resource if SBX-JK v31.gob is in a mod folder: a mod folder comes before Resource, as in the original game, so SBX's files replace this add-on's and there's no Dual Sabers key. (The other way round, SBX-JK v31.gob in Resource and this GOB alone in a mod folder, works, but the list names it after the folder.)

PLAYING TOGETHER
- Browser players can't be in the same game as desktop players (JK.EXE or OpenJKDF2) yet: the browser version's games go through its own lobby, which desktop games can't reach. Browser players play with browser players, and desktop players with desktop players.
- Everyone in a game needs SBX-JK v31.gob and this GOB, the same version of each. A browser player who joins a game hosted in the browser version gets the host's mods; a desktop player needs both GOBs in their own game folder.
- Each player's game shows the others as far as it can: a game that makes the reverse grip shows everything, and an older browser version shows a player in the reverse grip in Boc's stance.
- A game sends the other players its animations by their number in a list, so every game has to number them the same way. For each animation the browser version makes as a level loads, this GOB has a stand-in (3do\key\rt_*.key: 0.01 seconds long, moving nothing), which a game that can't make it loads instead. So every game's list is the same, and an animation one player's game sends is the same one on everyone's.

USE
Press your Dual Sabers key. From any weapon it takes out the Sith lightsaber, lit, in Boc's stance. Press it again to go back to the staff, and again for Boc's. Bind it first in Setup > Controls > Keyboard, where it's just after Lightsaber Stance.
Or take out the Sith lightsaber (5) and press your Lightsaber Stance key. The stances go: staff, Sariss, Boc's two sabers, the reverse grip, then back to the staff.
In Boc's stance:
- Fire, standing still: Boc's chop, then his cut, one after the other.
- Fire while moving: a twin saber attack for the way you're going (forward, back, or sliding left or right). Forward, walking or running, it's the twin cross-cut.
- Fire while standing and turning fast (drag the mouse left or right): a twin spinning slash that way. As with SBX's lightsaber, the turn has to be fast (over 200 degrees a second), which in practice takes the mouse, and it doesn't happen while crouching.
- Second fire (Z unless you changed it): the twin saber heavy cut.

GOOD TO KNOW
- In first person you still see SBX's staff. Use the third-person view to see both sabers.
- Like SBX's other stances, the blades hurt on any touch, not only during an attack.
- Like the lightsaber, it isn't switched away when you pick up a backpack or a weapon. Switching by hand works as usual.
- Each attack hits an enemy once, even if both blades touch it. A hit does the same damage as SBX's other Sith saber stances.
- Every attack swings its blades about as fast as SBX's other saber attacks. Boc's chop plays 1.1 times as fast as his own and his cut 1.4 times; the Sith Sabers left and right cuts play 1.3 times as fast as in Sith Sabers, its back cut 1.07 times and its heavy cut at its own speed. The twin spinning slashes take a little longer than SBX's (1.05 s turning left, 0.8 s turning right). The forward cross-cut takes a second, about as long as Sith Sabers' forward cut. Until 24 October 2026 the chop, the cut and the Sith Sabers attacks all played 1.5 times as fast, and at their fastest the chop, the back cut and the heavy cut swung faster than any of SBX's own.
- The Dual Sabers key is a 21st action key. Jedi Knight has room for 20, and SBX uses all of them, so it needs the browser version above, which has room for 32.
- Its static.jkl makes room for 256 scripts instead of SBX's 1000 (SBX uses about 50). In OpenJKDF2 each one takes about 1 MB, so this saves about 750 MB, and lets a player who only has SBX join a game with this add-on: the browser version has 2 GB in all.
- With Continuum (or another skin pack) ticked as well: the browser version (the site since 25 October 2026) puts the ticked mods' skin lists together, so you can pick from both (282 skins with Continuum and SBX), and their menu text, so SBX's controls and Continuum's skin names both show. Where both have a skin (Boc, Dengar, Exar Kun, Ki-Adi, Lando, Leia, Mace, Maw, Oola, Thrawn, Tusken and Xizor), the mod higher in the list gives its model. Continuum's skins are made on the original skeleton, without the extra joint SBX's staff puts its far blade on; that browser version works the joint out from SBX's own Kyle, so the staff has both blades on them. Boc's stance and the reverse grip work on any skin.
- Footsteps when walking: SBX 3.1's walk, walk-back and strafe animations have no footstep markers (it replaced them with saber damage ones), so walking made no sound in any stance. Its legs in them are the original game's, and the browser version gives each the original game's footstep markers back as it loads (misc\keymarkers.dat). Running already had them. Crouching and turning on the spot are silent, as in the original game.
- It replaces SBX's saberstance.cog, client_weapons.cog, weap_sithsaber.cog, kyle.cog, static.jkl, items.dat and jkstrings.uni with changed copies, and adds cog\dualsabers.cog, misc\keyretarget.dat, misc\keymarkers.dat, misc\pup\ky_bo.pup, ky_rg.pup and ky_rgd.pup, seven 3do\key\sbxd_*.key files, ten 3do\key\rg_*.key files and the 37 3do\key\rt_*.key stand-ins. Another mod that changes those files won't work alongside it.
